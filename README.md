## Featured projects

### 🌡️ [Berlin Heat Risk & Cooling Priority Explorer](https://github.com/Medhansh-Ahuja/berlin-heat-risk-cooling-priority-explorer)
A geospatial decision-support tool that maps surface heat across Berlin and ranks non-green hot zones where cooling interventions should be prioritised.
- Combined official Berlin Climate Analysis 2022 data (WFS), OpenStreetMap green spaces and district boundaries
- Found that green areas are **~1.0 °C cooler** on average than non-green areas
- Delivered as an interactive **Streamlit** dashboard with maps, KPIs and a priority table

`Python` `GeoPandas` `OSMnx` `Folium` `Streamlit`

### 🚦 [Berlin Road Safety SQL Analytics](https://github.com/Medhansh-Ahuja/berlin-road-safety-sql-analytics)
A complete SQL analytics pipeline on **11,267 Berlin road accidents (2021)**: raw loading, cleaning, data-quality checks, analytical views and exports.
- Mitte accounts for **15.6%** of all accidents; the peak hour is **15:00**
- Cyclists are involved in **~38%** of accidents, highlighting vulnerable road users
- Reusable SQL views for trends, district summaries and map exports

`PostgreSQL` `SQL` `Data Quality` `DBeaver`
